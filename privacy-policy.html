Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Information we collect

The figures you enter into the calculator are processed locally in your browser and are never sent to our servers. We do not require accounts. Like most sites, our hosting provider may log standard technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. We and our partners use cookies to keep the site working, measure traffic and show advertising. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may be affected.

Google AdSense and DART cookies

We use Google AdSense to display ads. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies — including the DoubleClick DART cookie — to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You may opt out of the DART cookie and personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or the Google advertising policy page.

Third-party ad vendors

Other third-party vendors and ad networks may place and read cookies or use web beacons on this site to serve and measure ads. We have no access to or control over these cookies. You can opt out of many vendors at aboutads.info or optout.networkadvertising.org.

Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

Where required by law, we request your consent before using cookies for personalized advertising and you may withdraw it at any time.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under GDPR and CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, delete or opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information. We do not sell personal information.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will post changes here. Questions? Use our contact page.
