Paycheck calculator FAQ

How is my take-home pay calculated?

We subtract estimated federal income tax (2026 brackets and standard deduction), Social Security (6.2% up to the $184,500 wage base), Medicare (1.45%, plus 0.9% above $200,000 single / $250,000 married), and an estimated state income tax from your gross pay.

What is the difference between gross and net pay?

Gross pay is what you earn before deductions. Net pay, or take-home pay, is what lands in your bank account after taxes and other withholdings.

Does this include 401(k), health insurance or other deductions?

No. Pre-tax deductions such as 401(k) contributions and health premiums will change your real paycheck. Use this as a baseline, then subtract your own deductions.

How is overtime calculated?

In Hourly mode, overtime hours are paid at 1.5× your hourly rate. Enter your regular hours per week and overtime hours separately.

How accurate are the state taxes?

State figures use a simplified effective flat rate, so they are estimates. States with progressive brackets, local taxes or special credits may differ. Check your state revenue department for exact figures.

Which pay frequencies are supported?

Weekly (52), bi-weekly (26), semi-monthly (24) and monthly (12) paychecks.
